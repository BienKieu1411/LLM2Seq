# S06 — DistillSpec: Improving Speculative Decoding via Knowledge Distillation

- Authors: Yuhui Zhou et al.
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08461
- Venue: ICLR 2024.
- Source role: peer-reviewed primary paper.

DistillSpec studies draft-target alignment through on-policy draft data and divergence choices tailored to the decoding task. It supports considering proposal-prefix/on-policy draft traces after the first EAL experiment. It does not establish that CE on this repository's traces or a particular encoder-decoder drafter will improve T5Gemma2 speed.
