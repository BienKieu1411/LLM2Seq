## 1. Executive Summary (`conclusion_first`)

**Yes — both `seam_grpo` and `seam_length_grpo` can be substantially improved across three orthogonal axes without changing the core SEAM/AFMR model architecture:**

1. **Systems & Compute Efficiency ($\sim 3.5\times\text{--}4.4\times$ reduction in Encoder/Bridge/Copy overhead with bit-identical gradients):**
   - Currently, for default `prompt_batch_size=1, group_size=4, gradient_accumulation_steps=8`, a single optimizer step executes `EviSeqAFMR.encode_source` (full HuggingFace Encoder + FP32 AFMR Bridge + `GroundedCopyHead.prepare`) **176 times** (32 rollout + 64 `SourceTeacher` + 64 checkpointed candidate forward/backward + 16 checkpointed gold CE forward/backward) and runs `align_copy_tokens` up to **96 times**, even though there are only **8 unique positive documents** and **8 unique negative documents** per step ([Finding F1](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/findings/F1_group_unique_source_encoding.md), [Source 16](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/16_local_systems_call_count_audit.md)).
   - Because `encode_source` has **zero dependence** on candidate `action_ids` or `labels` and executes in `eval()` mode (`dropout=0`), deduplicating unique sources per microbatch and indexing `(BridgeState, CopyState)` via `index_select(0, inverse_idx)` across the $G$ candidate trajectories and $1$ gold CE trajectory yields **bit-identical forward activations and mathematically identical parameter gradients** while cutting `encode_source` executions from **176 to 40 rows/step ($-77.3\%$)** (or **32 rows/step ($-81.8\%$)** when reusing `ref_model` log-probs in `SourceTeacher`).
   - Furthermore, fixing a latent `CopyState` cache-miss bug in `seam_vllm` (`SEAMModelState.copy_states` keyed only by `req_id` when vLLM's `EncoderCacheManager` skips re-encoding identical `mm_hash` features) both prevents a multi-step scheduler crash and unlocks `SamplingParams(n=G)` rollout on $B$ unique prompts ([Finding F4](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/findings/F4_trl_knobs_and_seam_vllm_hash_state.md), [Source 15](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/15_local_seam_vllm_state_audit.md)).

2. **Multi-Reward Variance & Teacher Signal Quality in `seam_length_grpo`:**
   - Under `scale_rewards="none"`, linear scalarization $R_i = Q_i + \eta S_i - \lambda C_i$ allows unbounded length cost $C_i = |L_i - W^*|/B_d$ to dominate bounded ROUGE quality $Q_i \in [0, 1]$ on tight-bucket datasets (`vlsp22` $B_d=34$, `vietnews` $B_d=38$): just **11.5 words** of length error difference reverses a **$+3.0$ ROUGE-F1 point** advantage, and a single verbose outlier candidate hijacks the group mean $\bar{R}_g$ ([Finding F2](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/findings/F2_multireward_variance_and_gdpo_gr3.md), [Source 13](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/13_local_reward_variance_audit.md)).
   - When `source_weight > 0`, `SourceTeacher` suffers from (a) extractive copy-gate bias (`GroundedCopyHead` assigns $-3.4 \times 10^{38}$ copy log-prob on low-Jaccard negative sources $x^-$, rewarding verbatim copying over abstractive compression), (b) $\tanh(\Delta/\tau)$ saturation ($\ge 0.987$ at $\tau=1.0$), and (c) a checkpoint ordering bug in `train.py:102` where `reference` is loaded from `sft_checkpoint` *before* `run_ce_warmup` ([Finding F3](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/findings/F3_source_teacher_efficiency_and_copy_bias.md), [Source 14](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/14_local_source_teacher_audit.md)).

3. **Objective & Advantage Conditioning via Installed `trl==1.16.0`:**
   - The installed `trl==1.16.0` already implements **DAPO Clip-Higher** (`epsilon_high=0.28`), **GSPO sequence-level importance ratios** (`importance_sampling_level="sequence"`), **CISPO** (`loss_type="cispo"`), **off-policy sequence masking** (`off_policy_mask_threshold`), and **GDPO** (`multi_objective_aggregation="normalize_then_sum"`), but `seam_grpo/config.py` currently filters them out in `Training.to_trl()` ([Finding F4](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/findings/F4_trl_knobs_and_seam_vllm_hash_state.md), [Source 17](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/17_local_trl_1_16_0_audit.md)).
   - Additionally, short-circuiting `SEAMActor._score_candidates` on rows where `advantages == 0` skips 100% of RL forward/backward compute on tied groups while preserving gold CE anchor gradients.

## 3. Deep-Dive Technical Analysis

### 3.1 Systems Bottleneck: Redundant `encode_source` & `align_copy_tokens` Across $G$ Candidates (H1)

In both [`src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/actor.py`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/actor.py#L46-L108) and [`src/seam_length_grpo/seam_length_grpo/teacher.py`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_length_grpo/seam_length_grpo/teacher.py#L42-L96), every microbatch of $B \cdot G$ candidate completions carries $G$ identical copies of `source_input_ids`, `source_attention_mask`, `copy_token_ids`, and `copy_token_mask` per prompt.

1. **Exact Per-Step Execution Count ($B=1, G=4, A=8, S=1 \implies 8$ unique prompts/step):**
   - **Collator & Copy Alignment (`LengthCollator.__call__` $\to$ `align_copy_tokens`):**
     - `SEAMGRPOTrainer._prepare_inputs` collates $A B G = 32$ rows ($32$ `align_copy_tokens` calls for $8$ unique sources).
     - `LengthVLLMRollout` passes $32$ rows to `SEAMProcessor._prepare_batch` ($32$ more `align_copy_tokens` calls).
     - `SourceTeacher.__call__` collates $32$ `negatives` rows ($32$ `align_copy_tokens` calls for $8$ unique negative sources).
   - **`EviSeqAFMR.encode_source` (`encoder` + FP32 `bridge` + `copy_head.prepare`):**
     - Rollout (`Rollout.generate` or `SEAMForConditionalGeneration.embed_multimodal`): **$32$ rows**.
     - `SourceTeacher.__call__` (`true_source` + `wrong_source`): **$64$ rows**.
     - `SEAMActor._score_candidates` (with `gradient_checkpointing: true` in [`actor.py:73-74`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/actor.py#L73-L74)): **$32$ forward rows + $32$ backward-recompute rows = $64$ rows**.
     - `SEAMActor._score_gold` (with `gradient_checkpointing: true` in [`actor.py:98-99`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/actor.py#L98-L99)): **$8$ forward rows + $8$ backward-recompute rows = $16$ rows**.
     - **Total:** **$176$ `encode_source` row executions per step** for only **$8$ positive and $8$ negative documents**.

2. **Why Group-Unique Encoding Is Mathematically & Numerically Exact:**
   - In [`Policy.train()`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/policy.py#L134-L142), `self.model.encoder.eval()`, `self.model.bridge.eval()`, and `self.model.copy_head.eval()` are enforced, so all dropout layers in `encode_source` are disabled.
   - `EviSeqAFMR.encode_source` ([`model.py:165-179`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/eviseq_new/eviseq_afmr/modeling/model.py#L165-L179)) depends exclusively on `(source_input_ids, source_attention_mask, copy_token_ids, copy_token_mask, prefix_embeds, prefix_attention_mask)` and has **zero dependence** on `action_ids` or `labels`.
   - Furthermore, `_score_candidates` and `_score_gold` use the **exact same source inputs** (`source_input_ids`, `prefix_token_ids`, etc.) for each prompt!
   - By deduplicating unique sources $U$ within `SEAMActor.forward` via `torch.unique(source_input_ids, dim=0, return_inverse=True)` (plus `prefix_token_ids` if present), running `encode_source` once on the $U$ unique rows (wrapped in a single `checkpoint` if `gradient_checkpointing=True`), and indexing `(BridgeState, CopyState)` via `.index_select(0, inverse_idx)` for each decoder slice (both candidate slices and gold CE slices), the backward pass accumulates:
     $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \theta_{\text{enc/bridge/copy}}} = \sum_{u=0}^{U-1} \left( \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}, u}}{\partial Z_u} + \sum_{i: \pi(i)=u} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{RL}, i}}{\partial Z_i} \right) \frac{\partial Z_u}{\partial \theta_{\text{enc/bridge/copy}}}$$
   - **Crucial Interaction with `trl==1.16.0` (`grpo_trainer.py:1388`):**
     `GRPOTrainer._prepare_inputs` calls `shuffle_sequence_dict(generation_batch)` across all $A B G = 32$ rows before slicing into $A=8$ microbatches of size $B G = 4$. If rows are shuffled individually, a microbatch of 4 rows can contain 4 different prompts ($U=4$ instead of $U=1$). Overriding the shuffle in `SEAMGRPOTrainer` to permute at the **group level** (shuffling the $A B = 8$ groups of size $G=4$, keeping each group's $G$ rows contiguous) preserves exact stochasticity across microbatches while guaranteeing $U = B = 1$ unique source per microbatch!

### 3.2 Multi-Reward Variance Dominance & `SourceTeacher` Biases in `seam_length_grpo` (H2)

1. **Numerical Proof of Length-Variance Dominance ([`reward.py:92-119`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_length_grpo/seam_length_grpo/reward.py#L92-L119)):**
   - With `scale_rewards: "none"`, `GRPOTrainer` computes group-centered advantages:
     $$A_i = R_i - \bar{R}_g = (Q_i - \bar{Q}_g) + \eta(S_i - \bar{S}_g) - \lambda(C_i - \bar{C}_g), \quad C_i = \frac{|L_i - W^*|}{B_d}$$
   - For `vlsp22` ($B_d = 34$) and `vietnews` ($B_d = 38$) with $\lambda = 0.10$, the marginal penalty per word of length error is $0.10 / 34 = 0.002941$ and $0.10 / 38 = 0.002632$.
   - Within a group of $G=4$ completions sampled from the same policy, weighted ROUGE F1 ($0.2 R_1 + 0.5 R_2 + 0.3 R_L$) typically has a standard deviation of $\sigma_Q \approx 0.01\text{--}0.02$ (range $\Delta Q \approx 0.02\text{--}0.035$).
   - **Failure Mode A (Trade-off Inversion):** Candidate 1 has higher ROUGE ($Q_1 = 0.350$) but misses `vlsp22` target length by 14 words ($C_1 = 14/34 = 0.412 \implies R_1 = 0.3088$). Candidate 2 has lower ROUGE by $3.0$ points ($Q_2 = 0.320$) but misses target length by 2 words ($C_2 = 2/34 = 0.059 \implies R_2 = 0.3141$). Candidate 2 receives a higher reward than Candidate 1, training the model to sacrifice content coverage for minor word-count adjustments.
   - **Failure Mode B (Verbose Outlier Hijacking):** If Candidate 4 in a group generates a 220-word runaway summary on a 50-word target ($C_4 = 170/34 = 5.0$, $\lambda C_4 = 0.50$), the group mean penalty shifts by $\lambda \bar{C}_g \ge 0.125$. All three remaining candidates ($i \in \{1,2,3\}$) receive large positive advantages ($A_i \in [+0.10, +0.16]$) regardless of which of the three actually had better ROUGE!

2. **Three Literature-Backed Reward Conditioning Upgrades:**
   - **Upgrade 2.1 — Clipped / Huber Length Cost (`max_band_cost`):**
     Cap per-candidate length cost in `LengthReward` at $C_{\max} = 2.0$ (or smooth $\tilde{C}_i = C_{\max} \tanh(C_i / C_{\max})$) and apply an optional deadzone $\tau_{\text{band}} \in [0, 0.15]$ so small variations well inside the target bucket do not override ROUGE F1 differences.
   - **Upgrade 2.2 — GDPO Decoupled Group Normalization ([`arXiv:2601.05242`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/06_gdpo_multireward.md)):**
     Instead of summing raw $Q_i$, $S_i$, and $-C_i$ before group centering, normalize each active component within the group using a variance-stabilized denominator $\sqrt{\text{Var}_g(X) + \sigma_{\min}^2}$ (or use TRL 1.16.0's built-in `multi_objective_aggregation="normalize_then_sum"`), ensuring length variance can never overwhelm ROUGE variance.
   - **Upgrade 2.3 — GR3 Multiplicative Quality-Gated Length Decay ([`ICML 2026, arXiv:2603.10535`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/07_gr3_icml2026.md)):**
     Provide an optional `"gr3"` reward mode where group-shifted quality $\tilde{Q}_i = (Q_i - \min_{j \in g} Q_j + \epsilon_0)$ is multiplied by length compliance $\exp(-\lambda \tilde{C}_i)$. Under GR3, a low-ROUGE summary ($\tilde{Q}_i \approx \epsilon_0$) cannot achieve a high reward solely by matching the target word count.

3. **`SourceTeacher` Biases & Checkpoint Bug ([`teacher.py`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_length_grpo/seam_length_grpo/teacher.py#L42-L96), [`train.py:102`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_length_grpo/seam_length_grpo/train.py#L102-L119)):**
   - **Bug in `train.py:102`:** `reference = load_actor(..., checkpoint_path=cfg.model.sft_checkpoint, ...)` is called **before** `warmup_checkpoint = run_ce_warmup(...)` (`train.py:109`). Thus, when `ce_warmup` runs inside `train.py`, `reference` (and `SourceTeacher`) uses the un-warmed SFT checkpoint whose `prefix_mlp` has never trained on length tags! Loading `reference` from `warmup_checkpoint` after `run_ce_warmup` fixes this mismatch immediately.
   - **Copy-Gate Extractive Bias:** Because `Negatives` chooses $x^-$ from the lowest 25% Jaccard pool, any token copied from $x^+$ has `copy_log_probs = -3.4e38` on $x^-$. Scoring `SourceTeacher` with vocab-only log-probs (or clamping per-token $\ell_t^+ - \ell_t^- \le 5.0$ nats) and raising `source_temperature` from `1.0` to `3.0` prevents saturation ($\tanh(\Delta/3.0)$ stays in the linear regime for $\Delta \in [1.5, 4.0]$) and removes the penalty against abstractive paraphrasing.

### 3.3 Algorithmic Knobs in `trl==1.16.0`, Zero-Advantage Short-Circuiting, and `seam_vllm` State Sharing (H3 & H4)

1. **Exposing Installed `trl==1.16.0` Capabilities in [`src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/config.py`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/config.py#L131-L168):**
   - `trl==1.16.0` already supports:
     - `epsilon_high: float | None = 0.28` (**DAPO Clip-Higher**, preventing entropy collapse on low-probability abstractive tokens).
     - `importance_sampling_level: str = "token" | "sequence"` (**GSPO**, stabilizing length-sensitive policy updates across 30–160 token summaries).
     - `loss_type: str` including `"dapo"`, `"dr_grpo"`, `"bnpo"`, `"cispo"`.
     - `off_policy_mask_threshold: float | None` (masking trajectories where vLLM rollout vs. HF actor log-probs diverge).
     - `top_entropy_quantile: float = 1.0` and `use_bias_correction_kl: bool = False`.
   - **Silent Bug Fixed Along the Way:** In [`src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/policy.py:210-216, 230`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/policy.py#L210-L230), `entropy` is currently computed under `with torch.no_grad():` and returned with `.detach()`. If `entropy_coef != 0.0` is ever set in TRL, the entropy bonus has **zero gradient**. Conditioning `no_grad()` on `not compute_entropy_with_grad` makes `entropy_coef` work as expected.

2. **Zero-Advantage Candidate Short-Circuit in `SEAMActor._score_candidates`:**
   - When a group of $G=4$ completions achieves identical rewards (or when individual rows have `advantages[i] == 0`) and `beta == 0.0` and `entropy_coef == 0.0`, the RL loss gradient for those rows is identically zero.
   - By passing `advantages` (available in `inputs["advantages"]` inside `SEAMGRPOTrainer._compute_loss`) or an `active_candidate_mask = (advantages != 0)` into `SEAMActor`, `_score_candidates` only runs decoder forward/backward slices on rows where `advantages != 0`, filling inactive rows with detached `old_per_token_logps`. When an entire group of $G=4$ ties, **100% of candidate forward/backward compute is skipped**, while `_score_gold` still updates the CE anchor!

3. **Fixing the Latent `seam_vllm` `CopyState` Bug and Enabling `SamplingParams(n=G)` ([`src/eviseq_new/seam_vllm/state.py`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/eviseq_new/seam_vllm/state.py#L83-L108)):**
   - In [`state.py:83-108`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/eviseq_new/seam_vllm/state.py#L83-L108), `SEAMModelState.embed_multimodal` stores `CopyState` in `self.copy_states[feature.request_id]`.
   - However, vLLM V1's `EncoderCacheManager` deduplicates encoder runs by `feature.identifier` (`mm_hash`). Whenever two requests share the same `mm_hash` and `embed_multimodal` is only invoked for the first request (which happens either when `SamplingParams(n=G)` is used OR when $G=4$ repeated requests are split across two scheduler steps!), `self.copy_states[req_id]` is missing for the subsequent requests, crashing `gather_copy_batch` with `RuntimeError: SEAM copy state missing for active requests`.
   - **Solution:** Store `self.copy_states_by_hash[feature.identifier]` alongside `self.copy_states[req_id]` in `SEAMModelState`, record `self.req_to_hash[req_id] = feature.identifier` when registering multimodal features, and clear `copy_states_by_hash` on `reset_encoder_cache()` / `sync_weights()`.
   - Once `copy_states_by_hash` is in place, `VLLMRollout` (`seam_grpo`) and `LengthVLLMRollout` (`seam_length_grpo`) can deduplicate contiguous group prompts and call `self.llm.generate(unique_prompts, SamplingParams(n=G, ...))`, reducing vLLM collator, encoder, bridge, and copy-alignment work by $G\times$ ($4\times$).

## 4. Phase 6.5 — Adversarial Self-Critique & Failure Mode Analysis

Before recommending implementation, we stress-tested every proposed improvement against potential regressions:

1. **Could deduplicating `encode_source` in `SEAMActor` change floating-point results or gradients due to batch-size sensitivity in LayerNorm / FlashAttention?**
   - *Critique:* On GPU, running an encoder on batch size $U=1$ vs. $B G = 4$ can occasionally differ by $\sim 10^{-7}$ in BF16 due to CUDA thread-block tiling, even though operations are row-wise independent.
   - *Mitigation:* In fact, with `score_micro_batch_size: 1` (the current default in both `grpo.yaml` configs!), `SEAMActor._score_candidates` **already** slices `source_slice` to batch size $S=1$ inside the loop (`for start in range(0, total, self.score_micro_batch_size)` at [`actor.py:67`](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/src/seam_grpo/seam_grpo/actor.py#L67))! Thus, encoding each unique source at batch size $1$ (or slice size $S$) matches the exact tensor shape currently passed to `encode_source`, guaranteeing **bit-for-bit identical** forward logits and gradients while avoiding $G-1$ redundant recomputations.

2. **Could group-level shuffling in `SEAMGRPOTrainer` alter training dynamics compared to TRL's row-level `shuffle_sequence_dict`?**
   - *Critique:* In `trl==1.16.0`, when `num_iterations == 1` (single epoch per rollout batch, which is the default in `seam_grpo` and `seam_length_grpo`) and `gradient_accumulation_steps = A`, gradients from all $A$ microbatches are accumulated **before** a single `optimizer.step()` is taken!
   - *Mitigation:* Because `loss.backward()` is linear across the $A$ accumulated microbatches before `optimizer.step()`, the order of rows across the $A$ microbatches within a single optimizer step has **zero mathematical effect** on the accumulated gradient $\sum_{a=1}^A \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_a$! Keeping each prompt's $G$ candidates in the same microbatch is strictly a memory-locality and compute-deduplication win with zero change to the optimizer step.

3. **Could GDPO (`normalize_then_sum`) amplify noise when all $G=4$ candidates have nearly identical length cost or ROUGE?**
   - *Critique:* If all 4 candidates in a group have almost identical word counts (e.g. $L \in \{50, 50, 51, 50\}$), standard-deviation normalization $\frac{C_i - \bar{C}_g}{\sigma_C + 10^{-8}}$ would blow up a 1-word difference into a $\pm 1.5\sigma$ penalty!
   - *Mitigation:* This is a critical catch. As noted in Dr. GRPO ([Source 02](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/02_dr_grpo.md)) and our local audit ([Source 13](file:///Users/kieugiangbien/Downloads/Project/LLM2Seq/doc/SEAM_GRPO_IMPROVEMENTS_RESEARCH_20261017/sources/13_local_reward_variance_audit.md)), any component-wise normalization must use a **damped scale floor** $\sqrt{\sigma_C^2 + \sigma_{\min}^2}$ (e.g. $\sigma_{\min} = 0.05$) or **Huber-clipped length cost** $C_i^{\text{clip}} = \min(C_{\max}, \max(0, C_i - \tau_{\text{band}}))$ so near-zero variance within a group smoothly shrinks the normalized signal toward zero rather than amplifying noise.
