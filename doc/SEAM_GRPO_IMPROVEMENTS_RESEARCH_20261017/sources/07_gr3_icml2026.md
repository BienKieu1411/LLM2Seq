Verified excerpt:
> "we present Group Relative Reward Rescaling (GR3), which reframes length control as a multiplicative rescaling paradigm, effectively establishing a generalized, continuous, and reward-dependent gating mechanism."

Relevance to SEAM GRPO (`seam_length_grpo`):
Analyzes the compensatory trade-off of additive length penalties ($R = Q - \lambda C_{\text{len}}$), where a model can trade off content quality $Q$ to reduce $C_{\text{len}}$ when $\lambda$ is too large, or ignore $C_{\text{len}}$ when $\lambda$ is too small. Proposes shifting group rewards to be non-negative relative to the group minimum before applying length-dependent gating so poor-quality candidates cannot win positive advantage solely by matching target length.
