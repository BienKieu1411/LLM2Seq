Verified excerpt, abstract: “additive penalties introduce a compensatory effect”.

GR3 uses multiplicative rescaling plus group-relative regularization and advantage calibration to address length inflation in RLHF/RLVR. It is counterevidence to declaring additive costs universally safe. Its adaptive length budgets are not the same task as matching an externally requested Vietnamese word count. Do not replace it with a naïve Gaussian multiplier and claim to implement GR3.
