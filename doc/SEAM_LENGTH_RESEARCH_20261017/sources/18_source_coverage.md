Verified excerpt, section 5.3: “larger scale of the coverage reward”.

The reward combines reference overlap with source extractive-fragment coverage, including evenness and length normalization. Some settings increase coverage while lowering ROUGE. This is evidence for scale calibration and against assuming more source coverage improves every metric. Its multi-document lexical coverage is not a measure of supported facts or budgeted semantic coverage for SEAM.
