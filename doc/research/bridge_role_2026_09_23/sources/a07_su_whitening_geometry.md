# A07 — Su et al. (2021), whitening sentence representations

- **Primary source:** Jianlin Su, Jiarun Cao, Weijie Liu, Yangyiwen Ou, “Whitening Sentence Representations for Better Semantics and Faster Retrieval,” arXiv:2103.15316 (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15316
- **Relevant evidence:** The paper describes contextual BERT vectors as anisotropic and reports a post-processing transform that removes a mean and applies a covariance-based linear transform. The exact whitening construction is `x_tilde = (x - mu) W`; see the paper's abstract/introduction and Sections 3.2–3.3 (arXiv HTML lines 3–5, 11–18, 59–76).
- **What it supports here:** A document-conditioned feature geometry operation can expose directions suppressed by a shared coordinate system. A masked common-direction correction is a deliberately much smaller bridge-only probe inspired by the mean-removal part; it is not a claim that sentence-embedding whitening transfers to summarization cross-attention.
- **Limitations and contrary evidence:** The experiments are sentence-similarity retrieval benchmarks and use statistics over an embedding set, not per-document token memories in an encoder–decoder. The paper's full covariance transform would add a potentially unstable, width-sized operation and is outside the proposed minimal probe. Therefore this source motivates a diagnostic, not expected task gains.
- **Use in verdict:** Treat “anisotropy/common direction is a plausible geometric bottleneck” as a hypothesis. Require direct measurements on `H0` and a short PPLX validation before full training; do not cite this paper as evidence of summarization improvement.
