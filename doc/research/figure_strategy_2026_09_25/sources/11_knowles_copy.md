# Knowles and Koehn (2018): copying and subwords

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/D18-1339/
- Type: original EMNLP neural translation study; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 2/5; low-bias 4/5.
- Short quote from abstract: “they even copy words that they have already learned they should translate”.
- Relevant observation: copy behavior depends on context and surface features as well as subword segmentation. A tokenizer-mismatch plot should control or stratify by term length/type and avoid interpreting every copied word as a success.
- Limit: neural translation is not document summarization and the study does not test an independently pretrained encoder/decoder pair.
