# Guo and Vosoughi (2023): output-length bias

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.984/
- Type: original EMNLP research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 4/5; low-bias 4/5.
- Short quote from abstract: “metrics exhibit a bias toward the length of generated summaries”.
- Relevant observation: a source-budget plot can change because longer visible input changes summary length. Log and show output length as a diagnostic and avoid treating ROUGE movement alone as better source use.
