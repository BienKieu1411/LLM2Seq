# Wan et al. (2025): positional faithfulness

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.442/
- Type: original NAACL research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 5/5; low-bias 4/5.
- Short quote from official abstract: “remain sensitive to document order”.
- Relevant observation: a positional intervention can change automated faithfulness judgments, so moving evidence is a more involved experiment than simply changing generator input length.
- Limit: its multi-document/order settings and evaluated models differ from SEAM. The study cannot predict a SEAM position effect.
