# Wu et al. (2024): long-document evaluator input

- Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.07382
- Type: original arXiv research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 4/5; recency 4/5; low-bias 3/5 (authors report their own evaluation method).
- Short quote from abstract: “performance increases as the size of the document decreases”.
- Relevant observation: this refers to evaluator–human correlation, not summarizer quality. The amount and selection of source passed to the evaluator can change measured performance.
- Limit: the source's GPT-based evaluation study does not establish the behavior of AlignScore or SCALE specifically.
