# Bishop et al. (2024): long-document factuality evaluation

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.941/
- Type: original LREC-COLING research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 4/5; low-bias 3/5 (introduces its own framework).
- Short quote from abstract: “these metrics have restrictive token limits”.
- Relevant observation: factuality evaluation over long sources is sensitive to how much source context the evaluator can inspect. Keep evaluator inputs and chunking fixed across a source-budget sweep.
- Limit: the paper is a critique and framework for evaluation; it does not validate AlignScore or SCALE on SEAM's outputs.
