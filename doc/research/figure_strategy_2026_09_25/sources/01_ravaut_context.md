# Ravaut et al. (2024): context utilization

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.153/
- Type: original ACL research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 4/5; low-bias 3/5 (authors evaluate their own benchmark).
- Short quote from abstract: “mapping facts from the source to the summary is not trivial”.
- Relevant observation: the paper studies position and input-context utilization in summarization across six LLMs and ten datasets. The indexed official PDF describes Figure 6 as long-input performance on arXiv and GovReport versus truncated maximum source length. This establishes a figure precedent, not an expected outcome for SEAM.
- Limit: the paper studies LLM inference and its own metric/protocol; SEAM is jointly fine-tuned and has a distinct source interface. Its source-position results should not be copied as a performance claim.
