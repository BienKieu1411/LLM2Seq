# Guo et al. (2022): LongT5

- Primary record: https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-naacl.55/
- Type: original Findings of NAACL research paper; accessed 2026-09-25.
- Credibility 5/5; recency 3/5; low-bias 3/5 (authors report their own model).
- Short quote from abstract: “explores the effects of scaling both the input length and model size”.
- Relevant observation: long-input summarization outcomes may change as the visible input increases. This supports a measured source-budget sensitivity analysis.
- Limit: LongT5 changes model architecture and pretraining as well as length; its performance does not predict the shape of SEAM's curve.
