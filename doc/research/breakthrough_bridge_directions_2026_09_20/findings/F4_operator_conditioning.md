# F4 — Conditioning the read operator is novel but weakly validated

Hypernetwork and instance-dependent adaptation papers show that small,
layer-specific modules can be generated from encoded inputs. None validates a
per-document PPLX-to-Qwen cross-attention operator. This direction has the
highest novelty and weakest task-specific evidence; it should follow a stable
positive bridge result.

Sources: `s05_hyperformer.md`, `s17_hint.md`, `s22_idpg.md`, `s23_iapt.md`,
`s19_prefix_limit.md`, `s20_local_evidence.md`.
