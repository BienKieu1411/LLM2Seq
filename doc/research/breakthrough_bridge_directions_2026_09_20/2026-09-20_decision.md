# Decision: four bridge directions worth testing

The full design report is in [`four_directions.md`](four_directions.md). The
local failures justify leaving the source-compression/write-back family rather
than tuning another region size, rank or gate.

The four retained hypotheses are:

1. per-decoder-layer, H0-anchored encoder-depth K/V;
2. an independently normalized gated side-memory branch;
3. decoder-query-dependent block-to-exact-token retrieval;
4. a document-conditioned low-rank cross-attention operator.

The first two experiments should be **layerwise coupled-depth K/V** and **gated
side-memory cross-attention**. They preserve exact token K/V and grounded copy,
start at the direct-projection function, use CE only, and have the cleanest
branch-off ablations. Query-hierarchical retrieval has stronger direct R-2
intuition but higher masking/cache risk. The hyper-operator has the most novelty
and the least task-specific evidence.

No source establishes that any candidate will improve PubMed ROUGE. This report
therefore records falsifiable designs and rejection criteria rather than a
performance promise. Select on validation and use a locked test evaluation only
after the architecture is fixed.
